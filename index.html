Privacy Policy for Rush Hour 3D

Bijay Chalise ("we", "us", or "our") operates Rush Hour 3D (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Rush Hour 3D.

1. Information We Collect

Rush Hour 3D does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or age.

The Game does not use external analytics services or tracking SDKs.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your Android device. This locally stored information is not transmitted to us.

2. Advertising

Rush Hour 3D uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display banner advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or receive certain information from your device for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and personalizing advertisements, depending on your device settings, consent status, and applicable laws.

This may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and app information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information

We do not directly access or control information collected by Google through its advertising services.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Advertising Identifier

The Game may use the Android advertising identifier through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising purposes.

You can manage or reset advertising-related settings through your Android device's privacy and advertising settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Rush Hour 3D does not operate its own servers for collecting player information.

However, Google AdMob is integrated into the Game and may process information as described above in order to provide advertising services.

5. Data Retention

We do not maintain a personal-information database for Rush Hour 3D.

Game progress stored locally on your device remains on your device until it is deleted by the Game, the app's data is cleared, or the Game is uninstalled.

Information processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's own retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Rush Hour 3D is a general-audience casual game and is not specifically designed or marketed as a children's app.

The Game does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Because third-party advertising services may process device-related information, parents and guardians should review Google's privacy and advertising policies if the Game is used by a child.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information through the Game.

Because Rush Hour 3D does not maintain its own user account system or personal-information database, we do not store player personal information on our own servers.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using the Game at any time by uninstalling Rush Hour 3D.

You may also manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google's available privacy controls.

9. Third-Party Services

Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google AdMob

Used to display banner advertisements.

Capacitor Android

Used to package the game's web-based Three.js application as an Android application.

Third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" shown at the top of this page.
